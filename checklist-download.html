Before You Read the Checklist

The Real Reason Buyers Lose Their Deposit

It's rarely because they didn't check anything. It's because they checked the information that's easy to find — not the information that actually determines risk.

🏭

You can't tell a factory from a trader

Alibaba's badges confirm a paid membership — not real production equipment. Trading companies routinely present themselves as manufacturers with polished photos of a factory they don't own.

⚖️

Lawsuits and debt are invisible to you

China's judicial and credit databases record pending litigation and enforcement actions — but none of it shows up on a company website, a sales deck, or an Alibaba profile.

🌐

The language barrier hides the truth

Government registries are public in principle — but they're in Chinese, use legal terminology that doesn't translate cleanly, and require domain knowledge most buyers don't have.

💸

Confidence, not caution, causes the loss

A buyer who knows they haven't verified anything is careful. A buyer who completed 6 "standard" checks and felt confident is the one who wires the deposit to a supplier that vanishes.

The pattern is consistent in documented cases: the buyer felt confident, made the deposit, and only afterward discovered the information that would have changed their mind — sitting in a government record, in Chinese, that no one thought to check.
